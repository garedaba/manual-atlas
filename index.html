MANUAL

Mapping Areal Networks Using Academic Literature

An open, living evidence matrix of anatomical connections in the primate brain, built from published tract-tracing and histological studies. Every connection links to its source papers (with page and figure locations) and is checked by an expert before it is published. Results can be viewed in any supported brain atlas.

Status: in development.

Data will be released under CC BY 4.0, with a Zenodo DOI for citation. Questions: open an issue.
